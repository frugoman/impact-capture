// how it works

Seconds now, instead of guessing in six months.

For senior engineers a big part of the job leaves no trace in Jira or GitHub. Impact Capture asks at the right moments and stays out of the way the rest of the time.

Check-ins at natural breaks

When you come back to your Mac after time away, and once at the end of the day. Never mid-flow, capped per day, and it backs off if you keep skipping.

Talk or type, from anywhere

Answer with Yes / No, a sentence, or your voice, transcribed on your Mac. A global shortcut (⌃⌥L) opens the quick-log box on any screen.

Plain Markdown you own

One file per day, in a folder you pick. Export any period as a review-ready summary, or point your AI tool of choice at the folder.

// menu bar

Your week at a glance.

Click the menu bar icon to log something, tag it, and see today's and this week's captures.

  • Categories like Unblocking, Influence, Mentoring and Risk spotted
  • Edit or delete anything, any time
  • Streaks, so the habit sticks
// review season

From scattered notes to evidence in one click.

Export the last two weeks, the quarter or the half as Markdown for your performance doc, a 1:1, or an AI tool. Settings has a ready-made prompt that turns your captures into performance evidence.

// the file format

Just text files. No lock-in.

Grep it, sync it, version it, read it in ten years. Each entry records when, how and why it was captured.
